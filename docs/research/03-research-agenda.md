# Research agenda: useful gains before novelty claims

Date: 2026-10-16. **Recommendation: run H2, then H7, then H6, after protocol and deployment gates.** H2 is a cheap-to-interpret replication that establishes the training baseline; H7 tests a plausible video-domain mechanism; H6 is the highest-risk mechanism study. These are proposed experiments, not implemented recipes or observed gains. No GPU was used and no training was started. [Related-work evidence](01-related-work-check.md) and [latency predictions](02-roofline-latency-model.md) constrain every recommendation.

## Expected-value ranking

Expected value here is a **decision judgment over (potential practical gain, remaining novelty, cost, falsifiability)**, not an estimated numerical expectation: effect probabilities and GPU-hours are unknown. “High/medium/low” are hypotheses, not measured results. Low novelty can still justify high practical priority. Costs include preprocessing, teacher/probe training, implementation and repeated runs, not just student updates. H6–H8 name the review's three stronger proposals. D1–D4 below are the additional directions identified in this check; this table covers every candidate proposed here.

**G2 — baseline/export audit.** With trained baseline outputs, separate FP32→ONNX-FP32 correctness, merged→unmerged equivalence, TRT-FP32 conversion, FP16 precision, and INT8 PTQ. ## One metric, data and replication contract

All quantities and hyperparameters below are **proposed settings**. Freeze configs, manifests, package/encoder builds and hashes before a run. A missing data/teacher asset blocks that experiment, not independent CPU work.

- **Splits:** training uses source-disjoint REDS train excluding REDS4; validation uses the separate REDS validation clips. Optional 1080p training sources require provenance and whole-title exclusion from validation/test. Final test uses held-out REDS4 and held-out native-1080p clips; all derivatives inherit source identity. E1/consumer clips cannot choose weights, stopping times or architecture. Contiguous windows, not every-fifth-frame samples, supply temporal training. Smaller-resolution REDS results are secondary to native 540p→1080p deployment quality. Insufficient staged clips make inference provisional; do not manufacture sample counts.
- **Spatial:** decoded RGB float [0,1], no undeclared modcrop; PSNR on full-range `Y=.2126R+.7152G+.0722B`, peak 1, explicit crop 0. SSIM uses the same Y, 11×11 Gaussian σ=1.5, constants .01²/.03², valid-window centers (5-pixel support exclusion stated). LPIPS-Alex receives RGB [-1,1], native resolution and pinned model/version. Save float engine outputs and evaluate consistently clamped/rounded display outputs separately. No published-score comparisons across conventions.
- **Temporal primary (T):** normalized RGB masked L1 of `[(S_t−W(S_{t−1}))−(G_t−W(G_{t−1}))]`. W and visibility are computed from GT only and frozen for every arm. Initial reproducible CPU flow baseline: Farneback on uint8 GT grayscale, parameters `(pyr_scale=.5, levels=3, winsize=15, iterations=3, poly_n=5, poly_sigma=1.2, flags=0)` in both directions. Backward flow B maps current pixel p to previous pixel `q=p+B(p)`; `W(A)(p)=bilinear(A,q)`. Forward flow F maps previous to current; require q in bounds and `||B(p)+F(q)||₂ ≤ 1+.05||B(p)||₂` HR pixels. For training crops also require all warp interpolation support inside the paired crop; do not sample padding as valid content. This is a confidence heuristic, not true occlusion ground truth. Report mask coverage and sensitivity to a second independently fixed reference-flow estimator before a strong mechanism claim. Never form transitions across scene cuts/clips. Report spatial quality on excluded/disoccluded pixels separately.
- **Diagnostics:** PSNR/SSIM, spatial LPIPS, raw warp error, and the current scalar adjacent-frame LPIPS difference, explicitly labeled ΔLPIPS diagnostic rather than attributed to an unopened metric definition. Predeclare reference-flow motion bins <1, 1–4, >4 LR pixels, low/high reference-gradient bins, visibility and newly visible regions. Do not let smoother outputs define the masks or the strata.
- **Aggregation/guardrails:** average frames within clips, then clips equally, then paired seed differences. Every temporal/KD pass requires clean PSNR loss ≤0.2 dB and spatial LPIPS increase ≤0.005, plus unchanged deployed latency within the empirically measured repeatability band. Use one-sided uncertainty bounds to check guardrails; point-estimate-only passes are provisional. Report all clips and strata; a temporal win bought with detail loss fails. These margins are proposed practical tolerances, not empirically established noise levels.
- **Replication:** pilot paired seeds **17,29,43**; independent confirmation seeds **71,101,131,173,211**, never pooled with tuning pilots. Match sample/crop/augmentation keys, initialization where applicable, updates and examples; save all RNG/sampler states. Report seed variability separately from clip variability. For confirmation, bootstrap the paired seed×clip result array by resampling seed rows and whole-clip columns independently (10,000 draws), retaining the full sequence within each clip; disclose limited five-seed precision and dataset dependence. Treat codec variants of a clip as a correlated group. Confirm at most one locked candidate per experiment; use one-sided 98.33% bootstrap lower bounds for the three primary contrasts (Bonferroni allocation of .05 across three), with approximate coverage explicitly acknowledged. Subset, secondary-endpoint and extra-arm claims are descriptive unless separately preregistered. Failure to exclude zero is inconclusive, not evidence of equivalence; five seeds do not guarantee adequate power.
- **Shared training:** Adam β=(.9,.999), no weight decay; normalized RGB Charbonnier ε=1e−3, LR patches 96×96 / HR 192×192, horizontal/vertical flips and 90° rotations shared across each window. FP32 training first; export all comparisons through the same merged TRT FP16 path. Training crops/codec states keyed by source/window/seed, no hidden `epoch_len` exclusion. Equal updates do not imply equal training cost: report wall time, examples and preprocessing/storage separately. No GPU-hour forecasts until representative throughput is measured.

## First: H2, with an initialization-matched control

The scientific question is whether **continued branch parameterization**, rather than its initial function, improves the chosen deployed graph. All arms use the same nonlinearities, global RGB residual and merged topology.

| Arm | Initialization and training |
|---|---|
| P0 | Ordinary plain-convolution initialization; train plain graph. |
| P1 | Initialize branches for this seed, immediately merge/copy their equivalent weights and biases, then train the plain graph. |
| R | Start from exactly the same branch initialization as P1; keep branches during training, merge only for evaluation/export. |

Verify P1 and R outputs at initialization agree in CPU FP32 to max absolute error <1e−4 on normal and tiny/border-heavy inputs, including nonzero biases. Any violation invalidates the comparison. P0 measures initialization effects; **R−P1 is the primary contrast**, R−P0 secondary. Do not claim matched parameters from matching an RNG seed alone.

Pilot each arm for **20k updates**, batch 16 frames, LR 5e−4 cosine to 1e−6. Also allow the same single LR alternative 2.5e−4 per arm using pilot validation only. Freeze one LR per arm, then train fresh confirmation runs for **100k updates** with the same cosine endpoints. Final checkpoint is the endpoint, not the best final-test checkpoint. Record equal-step quality and a separate validation learning curve at equal measured training time; this prevents slower branch training being called free training capacity.

**Pass:** confirmation mean PSNR gain R−P1 ≥0.05 dB, simultaneous lower bound >0, spatial/latency guardrails pass. **Stop:** failed merge/metric contract; pilot gain nonpositive at both LR settings with no upward late-validation trend; or excessive measured training cost for the available budget. A pilot stop is screening only. If R beats P0 but not P1, report an initialization/optimization result, not evidence that continued branching is useful. Three-arm pilot costs 180k student updates before LR alternatives; confirmation costs 1.5M updates, with runtime to be measured. No method novelty is claimed.

## Second: H7, isolate codec-state coherence

Keep the chosen **single-frame inference graph**. Train on paired adjacent outputs; temporal supervision needs no inference history. This tests a training mechanism before introducing a new VSR architecture.

Prepare four full-clip H.264 encodes per training source using pinned libx264, medium preset, yuv420p, fixed BT.709 conversion/tags, no B frames/scenecut, closed GOP length 16. Proposed four states: `(CRF, GOP phase)=(20,0),(20,8),(30,0),(30,8)`. For phase 0 encode starting at source frame 0; for phase 8 start at source frame 8. Retain only common source indices ≥16, discarding preroll outputs and preserving original timestamps. This creates shifted keyframe histories without inventing source frames. Set keyint=min-keyint=16, disable adaptive GOP changes, and verify actual frame types. This is a finite intervention in codec/QP/GOP state, not a full model of network rate-control dynamics. Encode before aligned crops.

For every source window draw uniformly from these four frame-indexed versions. **C** selects one state for the whole window; **I** selects independently per frame at the *same source index*. For two-frame windows, balanced 16-exposure blocks enumerate all 4×4 state pairs in I; C uses each diagonal pair four times. Thus one-frame empirical marginals match exactly per window over each completed block, not just CRF ranges. Shuffle exposure order with paired seed keys, and report incomplete-block discrepancies. Clean motion/HR pairing remains intact. Fix 50% clean and 50% compressed windows in every codec arm. The abrupt state changes in I are deliberately artificial and limit production-stream extrapolation.

| Arm | Degradation | Temporal training loss |
|---|---|---|
| C0 | Coherent C | Off |
| I0 | Matched-marginal stitched I | Off |
| C1 | Coherent C | On |
| I1 | Matched-marginal stitched I | On |
| F0 | One-frame encode augmentation, fixed color/downsample | Off; operational H3 control, different marginals |
| B0 | Clean bicubic only | Off; H3/clean-quality control |

All arms evaluate two adjacent frames, average their Charbonnier losses, and use batch 8 windows =16 frames. On arms add `λT_train`, the GT-relative masked temporal residual defined above. Choose λ from {.05,.1} on pilot validation, with the same search budget for C1/I1; lock a **shared** λ minimizing their average validation T subject to spatial guardrails. Start every arm from the same seed-paired clean baseline checkpoint, reset optimizer, and finetune **20k pilot / 50k confirmation updates**, LR 1e−4 cosine to 1e−6. Existing clean-baseline training is shared but counted in total project cost. Six base arms require 360k pilot updates; testing the second λ in C1/I1 adds 120k. Confirmation costs 1.5M updates, plus encoding/masks. Existing framewise loader cannot execute this experiment yet.

Evaluate unseen H.264 GOP **8/32/64**, CRF **23/35**, and a separate GOP-16 diagnostic at **the same CRFs**, on untouched source clips. For GOP G use source-relative phases `floor(kG/5), k=0,…,4`, created by encoding from that source index; evaluate the common source region starting at index 64 for every setting. Require ≥256 source frames or label the GOP-64 assessment unavailable; the currently staged 48-frame pieces are insufficient. This keeps phase/GOP comparisons on identical content. Evaluate AV1 using pinned SVT-AV1 preset 6, CRF **35/45**, intra period **32**, no lookahead for the causal playback track; validate actual encoder controls/build capability rather than assuming a flag produces low delay. Never severity-match using SR outputs. Retain all variants of a source clip together in uncertainty estimation.

**Primary contrast:** `J=(T_I1−T_C1)−(T_I0−T_C0)` on the equal-weight held-out H.264 configuration mean. Positive J indicates a coherence-specific temporal-loss benefit. **Pass:** J ≥5% of baseline T_I0, simultaneous lower bound for J >0, C1 beats I1 on T, and spatial/deployment guardrails pass. At negligible baseline T, a relative threshold is unstable: label that stratum inconclusive. AV1 transfer requires its own ≥5% T improvement and guardrails, reported separately; a H.264-only win does not establish codec transfer.

**Stop:** mismatched one-frame marginals, pairing/preroll bugs, mask failure, or pilot J≤0 / C1 temporal improvement over I1 <1% at both λ settings, or spatial guardrail violations at both. A substantial C0−I0 difference triggers an order/batch/distribution audit: the expected separable objective is identical, though gradient covariance/SGD can differ. An operational F0→C1 gain alone is insufficient, since severity and temporal loss both changed. Whole-frame shuffling is not the primary control: it would also destroy clean motion. If a later multi-frame extension is desired, repeat the factorial after D2's fresh latency gate.

## Third: H6, prefix predictability beyond teacher reliability

Do this only if the teacher/proxy pilot is affordable and distinguishable from ordinary confidence weighting. Broad student-adaptive KD is already established; compare to [Meta-KD](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.08356v1) before claiming more than an incremental ablation.

Freeze the same deployed one-frame student `X=x_t`; “prefix” here has one frame. A future causal 3-frame student is a separate preregistered extension. To make the first mechanism experiment explicit without assuming an available ×2 teacher checkpoint, a proposed teacher pair is **two offline five-frame concatenation CNNs**, input `[t−2,…,t+2]`, LR width 64, 12 internal 3×3 convolutions, RGB ×2 head with current-frame residual. T1 trains on the same clean/codec sources with Charbonnier; T2 adds `.01×LPIPS-Alex` to Charbonnier. Train each for 200k updates, batch 8 windows, Adam and 5e−4→1e−6 cosine schedule; freeze hashes before student comparisons. Both teachers receive identical HR centers and source-disjoint training data. This is a deliberately controlled teacher family, not a claim of state-of-the-art teacher quality. Published BasicVSR++/RealBasicVSR can be later teacher controls only with verified ×2 heads/checkpoints and a separately recorded recipe.

**Teacher gate:** on validation, T1 must exceed the baseline student by ≥0.1 dB PSNR and T2 improve LPIPS by ≥0.005 with ≤0.2 dB PSNR loss; otherwise stop/revise the teacher plan before student comparisons. Teacher training/precomputation is an additional 400k updates plus storage, not hidden in “training-only is free.” Clip endpoints use replicated available reference frames offline and are excluded from primary temporal subsets.

**Proxy:** split training source clips into three fixed folds A/B/C. Train a probe with exactly the deployed student graph on A for 50k updates; score B to fit a ridge regressor, then apply that regressor to C. Rotate roles three times, so neither probe nor regressor was fitted on the clips receiving those weights. Probe loss is mean absolute difference between high-pass probe output and high-pass mean-teacher output; high-pass is RGB minus a normalized separable 5×5 Gaussian (σ=1). Record 8×8-HR-patch errors. Ridge inputs are standardized current-LR patch luma mean, variance, gradient energy and Laplacian energy (standardization fitted on its own fitting fold); target is log(error+1e−6), penalty 1e−3. Define `predicted_error=max(exp(ridge_output)−1e−6,1e−6)` and `p=exp(−predicted_error/τp)`, with underflow to zero handled by the mass rule below. This is a deliberately cheap **texture-difficulty proxy trained against student errors**; four statistics do not directly identify missing future evidence. Keep probes/regressors out of the engine and never transfer their weights into only one arm. An anti-causal probe is deferred; the proposed mechanism claim is correspondingly limited.

Define teacher confidence `c=exp(−mean_patch|T1−T2|/τc)`, reference visibility v as the valid-pixel fraction per patch, and always retain GT loss everywhere. KD is the weighted mean absolute error to the selected teacher or the average of both; normalize weight mass over valid patches. K8 uses `e=mean_patch|(.5T1+.5T2)−GT|` and multiplier `exp(−e/τe)`. τc/τp/τe use the 25th/50th/75th percentiles of their respective disagreement/**predicted-error**/teacher-error training distributions. For every arm, cap the pilot search at three temperatures (diagonal percentile choices for combined masks) × KD multiplier {.05,.1}, i.e. six trials; select PSNR subject to guardrails, with lower multiplier as tie-break. Log all trial costs; they can multiply the base pilot budget by six. Freeze settings before confirmation. An all-zero weight window receives zero KD, not a NaN. Compute regression/exponentials in FP64 with finite-value checks.

| Arms | Purpose |
|---|---|
| K0; K1; K2 | GT only; T1 only; T2 only. |
| K3; K4; K5 | Uniform dual KD; visibility-only dual; confidence×visibility dual. |
| K6 | Confidence×visibility×predictability dual: proposed arm. |
| K7 | As K6, with predictability weights permuted within clip and matched bins of c, v, teacher-vs-GT error and GT gradient energy; preserve histogram/mass. Use training-only median splits, fixed before tuning; merge bins in fixed gradient→error→c→v order until ≥32 patches, then use a nonidentity cyclic permutation keyed by seed. Mark unswappable clips and report retained correlation. |
| K8 | Confidence×visibility×teacher-GT-error weighting; separates reliability from student predictability. |
| K9 | Student-conditioned Meta-KD comparator, same teachers/student/GT objective and capped validation budget; record its extra bilevel cost. |

All ten arms start from the same seed-paired baseline, with reset optimizer, batch 16 center frames, **20k pilot / 50k confirmation updates**, LR 1e−4→1e−6. Any extra probe warm-start or extra student updates must be given to controls too. Ten base arms cost 600k pilot / 2.5M confirmation student updates, plus temperature/loss searches, teachers and 150k probe updates per probe seed; all costs need measured throughput. K9 must be concretely implemented from the opened method before a superiority claim over it; if omitted, explicitly restrict the conclusion to K0–K8.

**Primary contrast:** K6−K5 PSNR on the equal-weight held-out H.264 GOP 8/32/64 × CRF 23/35 test mixture above, with confirmation mean gain ≥0.05 dB and simultaneous lower bound >0, plus spatial guardrails and no more than 5% degradation of T versus K5. **Mechanism claim additionally requires** beating K7 and K8 under paired uncertainty, a prespecified positive benefit in GT-defined high-motion/high-frequency visible subsets, and clear proxy response on the synthetic unavailable-information cases. Confirmatory K6 vs K7/K8/K9 superiority tests require a separately preregistered multiplicity allocation; without it they remain diagnostic. If K6 only beats uniform K3, report a selective-KD replication. If it loses to the best single teacher, there is no practical dual-teacher benefit. If it cannot beat Meta-KD, causal resolvability is not established as a new method.

**Stop:** teacher gate fails; out-of-fold proxy is indistinguishable from generic reliability/difficulty on controlled cases; pilot K6 fails to improve K5/K7; or actual teacher/probe budget is unaffordable. An unsuccessful proxy does not disprove that causal observability matters; it rejects this estimator/recipe. A successful proxy on synthetic ambiguity alone does not predict consumer-video gains.

## What can be settled without a GPU now

1. **Roofline arithmetic and graph accounting:** done for document 02; a second independent calculation reproduced all nine rows and identified the direct-convolution/Winograd caveat. CPU code inspection settles current model depth, channels, residual and shuffle placement. It cannot settle real TensorRT latency.
2. **Codec-order null:** exact algebra gives identical full-batch gradients after paired-sample permutation for a separable stateless student. A CPU arithmetic fixture also checks it. Batch/order-dependent optimization can still differ. Thus current framewise Charbonnier training cannot identify temporal coherence from ordering alone.
3. **Unavailable information:** CPU toy HR signals `(.7,.3,.6,.4)` and `(.3,.7,.4,.6)` both give `(.5,.5)` under two-sample box downsampling; a cyclic one-HR-sample shift gives distinct LR `(.45,.55)` and `(.55,.45)`. The present-only deterministic student cannot distinguish them; future evidence can. This is a constructed ambiguity, not a video result. Squared teacher matching has irreducible conditional variance. Input-only weighting cancels from the unrestricted conditional optimum; privileged/reference-dependent weights can change it. Neither teacher agreement nor visibility proves recoverability.
4. **Quantization and merge:** CPU scalar example at unit quantization scale, `round(.49+.49)=1` but `round(.49)+round(.49)=0`, proves noncommutation. It cannot prove useful INT8 quality recovery. Existing `merge()` deletes branches and uses `no_grad`; merged-aware training needs a differentiable equivalent-kernel forward, not repeated destructive merges.
5. **H2 mechanism:** for a differentiable equivalent kernel K(θ), small-step SGD induces approximately `ΔK=−η JJᵀ∇K L`, versus ordinary plain-kernel SGD. Matched initial functions do not match optimization geometry. Adam adds further differences. This explains the need for P1 and optimizer controls without asserting any quality gain.
6. **Protocol work:** CPU tests can validate metric/color fixtures, exact frame keys/shapes, split/derivative exclusion, sequence boundaries, balanced codec marginals, merge equivalence, and reproducible resume. These are meaningful correctness checks, not evidence for video quality or 60 FPS. No such repairs were made by this documentation task.

The CPU fixtures here establish small mathematical counterexamples only. GPU performance, codec-generalization effects, seed variance, teacher superiority, proxy validity, and engine precision recovery remain **unmeasured**.
